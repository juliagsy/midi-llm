\section{Limitations}

\paragraph{Small instruct split.}
The pilot MIDI-Instruct train split ($\approx$240 items) limits absolute editing performance. We explicitly frame S3 results as \emph{comparative} across arms.

\paragraph{Partial curriculum.}
Only S3 LoRA is trained in v0.1. Zero-shot and S2-only baselines may underestimate arm potential. Full S0--S2 curriculum is future work.

\paragraph{BPE-text vs.\ vocab extension.}
We do not compare against MIDI-LLM's vocabulary-extension recipe~\cite{wu2026midillm}. Our protocol trades dedicated music tokens for implementation simplicity and fair BPE-length measurement; a hybrid study is left open.

\paragraph{ABC arm excluded.}
ABC remains a popular symbolic format~\cite{kumar2026abc,zhao2025abceval} but is disabled until a validated MIDI$\leftrightarrow$ABC converter ships.

\paragraph{Generation metrics deferred.}
Caption-to-MIDI FAD/CLAP and MidiCaps generation tables are deferred to avoid metric disagreement dominating the narrative before editing results are established.

\paragraph{Synthetic unique-gold only.}
Eval items use invertible programmatic transforms. Descriptive edits with many valid outputs (constraint-gold) are Paper~3 scope.

\paragraph{Compute.}
Full 3-arm $\times$ 3000-step runs require $\approx$12--18 GPU-hours on T4; larger backbones are not explored.
