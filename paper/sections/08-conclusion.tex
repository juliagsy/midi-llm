\section{Conclusion}

We introduced MIDI-LLM, a controlled comparison framework for REMI+, AMT, and Octuple inside a unified Llama LoRA pipeline with a shared BPE-text serialization protocol. By evaluating on MIDI-Instruct's dual-metric editing benchmark alongside Llama-BPE efficiency statistics, we connect representation design to a task that generation-only papers cannot measure. Version~0.1 establishes the infrastructure---round-trip fidelity, reversible Octuple serialization, seeded reproducibility, and Colab training---and positions Paper~3 to scale editing with the best-performing arm on constraint-gold and descriptive instructions with human evaluation.
