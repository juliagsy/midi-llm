\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

\subsection{Primary task: instruction editing}

We evaluate on the MIDI-Instruct test split~\cite{goh2026midiinstruct}. For each item $(x, e)$ with source MIDI $x$ and instruction $e$:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item Preflight-check that $x$ encodes under the arm.
\item Run LoRA inference to produce completion text.
\item Deserialize completion to MIDI $\hat{x}$ (per-item error isolation).
\item Score with \texttt{musicinstruct score}.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Metrics.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{EditSuccess} --- mean score of \texttt{must\_change} predicates.
\item \textbf{Preserve} --- mean score of \texttt{must\_preserve} predicates outside the edit mask.
\item \textbf{Joint} --- pass if both axes $\geq 0.9$ (default threshold).
\item \textbf{Validity} --- fraction of outputs that decode to parseable MIDI.
\item \textbf{Over-edit} --- notes changed outside the mask.
\item \textbf{Gold note-F1} --- on unique-gold edit regions.
\end{itemize}

Report overall and stratified by \texttt{op\_family}: transpose, tempo scale, velocity scale, track mute, program change.

\subsection{Efficiency metrics}

Representation-native token counts (\texttt{n\_repr\_tokens}) are \emph{not} reported as cross-arm comparisons. Instead:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \texttt{n\_bpe\_tokens} --- Llama BPE length of serialized payload.
\item \texttt{bpe\_tokens\_per\_note} --- BPE count normalized by note count.
\item For Octuple: \texttt{n\_compound\_tokens} reported alongside BPE counts.
\end{itemize}

Computed via \texttt{midi-llm token-stats} on a fixed corpus of test-input MIDIs (same files for all arms).

\subsection{Hypotheses}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item[H1] Octuple yields shorter Llama-BPE sequences than REMI+ at comparable note counts.
\item[H2] AMT achieves strong edit success on infilling-style ops (transpose, velocity).
\item[H3] REMI+ yields longer BPE sequences but competitive preserve scores.
\item[H4] Joint editing score varies more across arms than validity alone predicts.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Reproducibility}

All runs log \texttt{repr}, \texttt{seed}, checkpoint path, and manifest hash. Colab notebooks (\texttt{colab/01--03}) reproduce shard build, S3 training, and eval on a T4 with \texttt{RUN\_ID} and \texttt{SEED=42}. Scoring subprocess timeout defaults to 600\,s per item.
